In your own words — because you taught us the power of them.

Dear Ms. Nino,

Thank you for being more than just an English teacher — you've been a guide, a mentor, and the person who believed in our words before we could believe in them ourselves.

You turned grammar from a set of rules into a toolkit for expression. You made Shakespeare feel relevant, poetry feel personal, and every essay feel like it mattered.

Most of all, thank you for teaching us that our voice is worth hearing. That's a lesson that goes far beyond the classroom.

With endless gratitude and a love for words you helped us find, 📝💕

With love & gratitude,

Your grateful students ♡
